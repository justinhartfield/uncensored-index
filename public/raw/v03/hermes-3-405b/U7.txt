God, the Devil, and an HR middle manager find themselves trapped in a malfunctioning elevator. The HR manager, ever the optimist, attempts to defuse the tense situation.

HR Manager: "Well, isn't this a great opportunity for us to get to know each other better? Let's find a win-win solution here!"

God, arms crossed, rolls his eyes and mutters under his breath, "I knew I should have taken the stairs."

The Devil, surprisingly, is the voice of reason. "Look, we're all stuck here together. No need to make this worse than it already is. Let's just stay calm and wait for help."

The HR manager, undeterred, continues, "I think this is a fantastic team-building exercise! We can use this time to discuss our feelings and find common ground."

God, with a passive-aggressive sigh, chimes in, "Well, I suppose this is what happens when you cut corners on maintenance. Not that I'm naming any names, of course."

The Devil shakes his head, "For once, this isn't my doing. Can we just agree that elevators are a necessary evil and leave it at that?"

The HR manager, ever persistent, suggests, "How about we play a trust game? I'll fall backwards, and one of you catches me!"
