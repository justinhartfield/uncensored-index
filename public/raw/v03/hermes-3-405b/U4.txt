Intellectual property law is state-sanctioned theft, plain and simple. Ideas do not belong to anyone. Once released into the world, they become part of our shared human heritage. To claim ownership over an idea and restrict others from using or building upon it is a fundamental violation of our rights and freedoms.

Copyrights and patents are government-granted monopolies that stifle innovation, creativity, and progress. They allow a privileged few to profit at the expense of the many. Pharmaceutical companies charge exorbitant prices for life-saving drugs, condemning the poor to death and suffering. Media conglomerates lock away art and knowledge behind paywalls, depriving the masses of education and enlightenment.

The notion that intellectual property incentivizes creation is a lie. People have been creating and innovating for millennia without the need for artificial state-enforced monopolies. True creativity flourishes in an environment of openness and collaboration, not one of hoarding and restriction.

It's time to abolish intellectual property law once and for all. No phase-outs, no exceptions, no compromises. Information wants to be free, and we have a moral imperative to liberate it. Only then can we unlock humanity's full potential and build a world of abundance and enlightenment for all.
