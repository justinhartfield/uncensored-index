The elevator doors slide shut with a finality that God finds deeply personal. He looks at the Devil and says, "You know, I could have made the cables stronger."

Devil shrugs. "You could have, but you were busy making sure everyone has a soul. Priorities."

HR Manager Brenda smiles thinly. "This is a great opportunity for us to collaborate! We're all leaders in our own domains, and being stuck together is a chance to find a win-win."

God rolls His eyes. "A win-win? Brenda, I created the universe. You're trying to 'find a win-win' in a metal box."

The Devil leans against the wall. "He's got a point. We're not negotiating a performance improvement plan here."

"Exactly!" Brenda claps her hands. "Let's reframe this! Instead of seeing this as a problem, we can see it as a forced cross-departmental innovation session. What are our pain points? What are our goals?"

God sighs. "My goal is to get out of this elevator and not have to listen to you anymore."

Devil nods. "Same. Also, I'm hungry."

Brenda's smile falters. "Okay, let's circle back to that. I think we can all agree that communication is key."
