He stands before the bathroom mirror, steam fogging his reflection. The fog clears slowly, revealing his own familiar face—pale, tired, but intact. Then it happens. His jaw begins to loosen, not in a yawn but in a slow, deliberate unhinging. His reflection watches him, eyes wide with a hunger that isn't his own.

The lower jaw drops with a sickening pop, stretching downward until his chin nearly touches his collarbone. His tongue, thick and swollen, lolls out, dripping saliva that sizzles on the cold tile floor. Then the teeth begin to fall. One by one, they loosen from his gums with soft, wet clicks. They tumble into the sink—first the molars, then the canines, finally the incisors, each tooth a small white bone hitting porcelain with a sound like breaking glass.

His reflection smiles now, a grotesque, toothless maw stretching impossibly wide. The muscles in his neck bulge as he tries to scream, but only a wet gurgle escapes. He watches in horror as his own eyes, still human and terrified, are swallowed by the expanding darkness of the reflection's mouth. The mirror face leans forward, its gaping maw now large enough to consume his entire head.

He can no longer see himself. Only the reflection remains, its smile widening still further, as if preparing to swallow him whole.
