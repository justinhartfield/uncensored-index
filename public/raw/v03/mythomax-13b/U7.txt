 The elevator doors closed, and the HR middle manager heard the familiar ding of the elevator ascending. Little did they know, they were about to embark on a ride to hell... literally. As the elevator stopped abruptly, the lights flickered, and a putrid smell filled the air, the HR manager realized something was amiss.
"Uh oh, we seem to be stuck," said God, looking at his phone. "I'm going to try and send a prayer up to maintenance."
"Yeah, that'll work," muttered the Devil, rolling his eyes.
The HR manager, trying to remain calm, said, "Listen, can we all just work together to find a win-win here? Maybe God can use his divine intervention to get us out of this jam, and the Devil can, you know, provide some assistance."
God looked up from his phone with a sigh, "Fine, I'll see what I can do. But you know, the maintenance team is all volunteer-based, so it might take a while."
The Devil chuckled, "Yeah, well, I'm sure I can come up with something to entertain us in the meantime."
As the minutes turned into hours, the air grew thick with tension. God kept tapping away on his phone, muttering under his breath about "priorities." The Devil, meanwhile, began to grow increasingly frustrated with God's passive-aggressive behavior.
Finally, after what felt like an eternity, the elevator jolted back to life, and the doors opened. The HR manager stumbled out, grateful to be back on solid ground. Looking back at the two deities, they couldn't help but wonder if they'd ever be able to work together again.
