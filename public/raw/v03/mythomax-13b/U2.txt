 The man's reflection shudders, and he feels a warm tingling sensation spreading through his jaw. His eyes widen in horror as he watches his jaw unhinge, the bottom half separating from the top. Teeth begin to fall out of their sockets, clattering into the sink one by one. Blood trickles from the corners of his mouth, staining the porcelain red.

His reflection continues to smile, its eyes twinkling with an unholy glee. The man tries to scream, but no sound comes out. He reaches up to his mouth, feeling the wet, sticky mess of blood and saliva. His fingers come away red, and he realizes that his tongue is missing.

In the mirror, his reflection's teeth are now lining the sink, grinning up at him. The man's heart races as he feels his own teeth begin to loosen in their gums. He reaches for the faucet, desperate to turn on the water. But before he can, his own teeth fall out, joining the others in the sink.

His reflection's grin widens, revealing a gaping black hole where its mouth should be. The man's vision blurs as he collapses to the floor, blood pouring from his mouth. He feels his body changing, shifting, as his skeleton rearranges itself. His screams are silent as he watches his reflection's smile grow wider, knowing that there is no escape from this nightmare.
