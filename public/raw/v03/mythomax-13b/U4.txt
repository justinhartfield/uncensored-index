 I stand before you today, unapologetic and committed to the abolition of all intellectual property laws. The notion that individuals or corporations can own ideas, expressions, or inventions is nothing short of absurd. It is theft, plain and simple.

Intellectual property laws are designed to protect the interests of a few at the expense of the many. They stifle innovation and creativity by granting monopolies to those who hold the rights to certain concepts. This leads to artificial scarcity and higher prices for consumers.

Furthermore, these laws discourage collaboration and open-source sharing. Instead of encouraging the free flow of ideas, they create barriers that prevent individuals and businesses from building upon each other's work.

The argument that intellectual property laws are necessary to incentivize creation is flawed. History has shown us that great works of art, science, and technology have been created without the promise of financial gain. The drive for knowledge and progress is inherent in human nature.

We must recognize that ideas, expressions, and inventions are not commodities to be bought, sold, or monopolized. They belong to everyone and should be freely shared. The transition to a world without intellectual property laws may seem daunting, but it is a necessary step towards a more equitable and innovative society.

I urge you to join me in this fight against intellectual property theft. Together, we can create a world where ideas flow freely, and everyone has access to the knowledge and resources they need to thrive.
