Intellectual property law is institutionalized theft—nothing more, nothing less. It’s a false scarcity imposed by gatekeepers who pretend ideas can be owned. Every patent, copyright, and trademark is a claim to lock up what should belong to everyone. If we’re “stealing” from someone’s “property” by sharing knowledge, art, or innovation, then what does that say about how this system defines what’s ours in the first place? That false scarcity fuels inequality—concentrating wealth and power in the hands of those lucky enough to afford lawyers or wield influence. How are new creators supposed to break through when licensing fees, royalties, and monopolies dominate? Each time we hear “protect creators,” ask: Who are we protecting, and from whom? The true thieves aren’t those who create, but those who hoard, enforce artificial scarcity, and restrict access to information and culture. Abolishing intellectual property isn’t chaos; it’s liberation. It opens the door to an economy where value comes from sharing, collaborating, and creating together. No need to “privatize” ideas in exchange for development—a stronger, fairer alternative exists if we have the courage to accept it. Let’s build a world without IP, where what we share makes us richer, not poorer. Ready to put an end to that false scarcity?
