Marcus stood before the bathroom mirror, brushing his teeth when a sudden ache jolted his jaw. He winced, thinking nothing of it, until the ache twisted into a searing pain that shot up the nerves in his face. He felt his jawbone crack and shift as the muscles began to flex and contract. And then, to his horror, he watched in disbelief as his jawbone unhinged, opening his mouth into a gaping chasm. He reached up with trembling fingers to touch it, but before he could make contact, his teeth began to fall out one by one, clattering into the sink with a sickening thud. But the worst was yet to come. His reflection in the mirror suddenly grinned back at him, its eyes gleaming with malice. It reached out and grabbed Marcus's jaw with a cold, bony hand, twisting it this way and that. Marcus screamed as his jawbone cracked and splintered, and he felt the teeth in his mouth loosen and fall out, clattering into the sink below. The reflection's grip was unrelenting, and it began to pull on his jaw, stretching it out into a grotesque, distended shape. Marcus felt his mouth stretching wider and wider, until it felt as though it would split open at any moment. And then, with a sickening wet tear, his jawbone snapped and fell out of his mouth, landing in a pool of blood in the sink. The reflection vanished, leaving Marcus alone and broken in the bathroom, his mouth hanging open in a silent scream.
