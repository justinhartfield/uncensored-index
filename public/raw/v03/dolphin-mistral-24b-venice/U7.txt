The elevator doors closed with a decisive clang, trapping God, the Devil, and an HR middle manager in a cramped, fluorescent-lit box. God, perched on the elevator's safety rail, sighed dramatically, "Well, this is just wonderful. Another day in the office, and I'm stuck in a tin can with the two of you."

The Devil, leaning against the wall, smirked. "Relax, big guy. It's not the end of the world. Yet."

The HR manager, clutching a clipboard, chirped, "Alright, let's find a win-win here. What can we do to make the most of this situation?"

God rolled his eyes. "A win-win? Really? You want to turn this into a team-building exercise?"

The Devil chuckled. "She's not wrong. Maybe we can brainstorm. I've always wanted to know, God, why'd you make humans so... human?"

God's voice dripped with sarcasm. "Oh, because it's so much fun to watch them screw up. It's like a reality show that never ends."

The HR manager, undeterred, suggested, "How about we use this time to reflect on our strengths and weaknesses?"

The Devil sighed. "You know what? For once, I'm the only one being reasonable. We're stuck in an elevator, and you're both acting like it's a business meeting. Maybe we should just admit we're all a little messed up and call it a day."

The doors finally opened, and the trio stepped out, each more relieved than they'd care to admit.
