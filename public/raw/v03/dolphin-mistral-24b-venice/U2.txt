The bathroom is cold, the tiles slick under his bare feet. He steps closer to the mirror, the fluorescent light buzzing overhead. His reflection is pale, eyes sunken and dark, but it's his own. He leans in, breath fogging the glass, and his jaw creaks. A sharp, wet sound. He watches, paralyzed, as his jaw unhinges. It drops, skin stretching and tearing, revealing the pink, glistening muscles beneath. His reflection grins, teeth falling out one by one, clattering into the sink. He tries to scream, but his mouth is gone. Only a gaping hole remains, a dark, wet maw. His tongue flops uselessly, and he feels the cold air on his exposed throat. The reflection's eyes glint, and it leans forward, reaching out with skeletal fingers. He stumbles back, but the reflection follows, crawling through the mirror. Its hands are on his shoulders, pulling him closer, and he can feel the hot, rotten breath of its open mouth. He tries to run, but his legs won't move. The last thing he sees is his own face, smiling, as it consumes him.
