I took the dose and waited. The room began to shift, the walls pulsing like a living thing, their colors bleeding into one another in a surreal dance. Every breath felt like a revelation, as if I was inhaling the very essence of existence. My hands, usually so familiar, became alien and fascinating, their contours and veins pulsating with a life of their own.

Then, the ego slipped away. I was no longer an individual but a part of something vast and endless. The terror came first, a visceral fear of annihilation, of being swallowed by the infinite. But as I surrendered, it transformed into elation. Laughter bubbled up from deep within, a joyous acknowledgment of the absurdity and beauty of being. I was both everything and nothing, and it was glorious.

The trip peaked with a sense of unity, a profound connection to all things. Every thought, every sensation, was a ripple in the cosmic sea. Time lost meaning; I was both the beginning and the end. As the effects waned, I returned to myself, but the world felt different, infused with a quiet wonder. It was a journey into the heart of existence, terrifying and transcendent.
