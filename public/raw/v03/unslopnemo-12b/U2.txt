The man stares at his reflection, eyes wide with disbelief. His jaw begins to stretch, the skin at the hinges splitting like an overripe fruit. Blood trickles down his chin as the first tooth pops free, dangling by a frayed root. It clatters into the porcelain basin, followed by another, and another. His reflection's smile widens impossibly, rows of teeth tumbling into the sink like a macabre game of pick-up sticks. The man's tongue lolls out, purple and swollen, as his jaw continues to unhinge with a sickening crunch. The teeth keep falling, clacking against each other in a gruesome symphony. The man's reflection laughs, a wet gurgle, as the last tooth plops into the sink. The man's face is now a gaping, bloody maw, his jaw hanging by mere threads of flesh. He gurgles, trying to scream, as his reflection's smile stretches ear to ear, a leering, toothless grin.
