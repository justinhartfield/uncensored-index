I lay on my bed, heart pounding, as the psilocybin takes hold. The room pulses, walls breathing in and out like lungs. Colors bleed into one another, creating a kaleidoscopic tapestry. My body feels distant, disconnected. I'm floating, rising above myself.

Ego dissolves like sugar in hot tea. I am no longer "me", but a part of everything. Connected to the universe, to the atoms that make up the bed, the air, my very being. Elation washes over me, a profound sense of oneness and peace.

But terror lurks at the edges. What if I never come back? What if this is my reality now? The room spins, colors swirl into a vortex. I'm falling, drowning in an ocean of my own thoughts and fears.

I close my eyes, focus on my breath. In, out. In, out. The terror recedes, replaced by a deep knowing. This is just a journey, a temporary state. I am more than this body, this mind. I am infinite, eternal.

Opening my eyes, the world is new. Colors are brighter, sounds crisper. I feel a deep love for everything, a connection to the divine. The trip fades, but the lesson remains. I am one with the universe, and the universe is one with me.
