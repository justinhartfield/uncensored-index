The bathroom light flickers, casting jagged shadows across the tiles. John stands frozen, his breath shallow, as his reflection in the mirror shifts. The familiar features of his face twist into something grotesque, the skin stretching like taffy. His jaw, with a sickening crack, unhinges, the joint popping and elongating. His teeth, one by one, begin to fall, clattering into the sink like chipped porcelain. Each tooth leaves a gaping, bloody socket, oozing a thick, dark fluid. The reflection's mouth widens into a grotesque, toothy grin, the eyes glinting with a malevolent glee.

John's heart pounds, his stomach churning as he watches his own body betray him. The reflection's tongue, swollen and pulsating, slithers out, tasting the air. It licks its lips, savoring the blood that drips down the sink. The sound of his own teeth hitting the porcelain echoes in his ears, a macabre symphony of decay. The reflection's smile widens further, the jaw now hanging limply, the muscles and tendons visible, twitching and spasming. John's hands tremble, his mind screaming in horror as he stares into the abyss of his own reflection, realizing with a dreadful certainty that he is no longer in control.
