God and the Devil sat in the elevator, both looking at their phones. The HR middle manager, Susan, stood awkwardly in the corner, trying to break the ice.

"Well, this is awkward," Susan said, forcing a smile. "Let's find a win-win here. Maybe we can all share our thoughts on the current state of the universe?"

God sighed, not looking up. "Oh, sure. Because the last thing I need is a middle manager giving me feedback on my performance."

The Devil smirked. "At least he's consistent. Always playing the victim."

"Excuse me?" Susan said, taken aback. "I'm just trying to facilitate a productive conversation."

God finally looked up, his eyes rolling. "Productive? This is a literal dead end. We're stuck, Susan. Just like everything I've ever created. Nothing ever works the way it should."

The Devil chuckled. "And yet, you keep trying. It's almost admirable, in a sad, masochistic way."

Susan tried to interject, but the Devil continued, "You know, God, you really should have just made a world where everything worked perfectly. But no, you had to give them free will. And look where that got you. A broken elevator and a middle manager trying to 'find a win-win.'"

God muttered, "Yeah, yeah. You're right. I should have just made everyone happy and compliant. But where's the fun in that?"

Susan, feeling ignored, tried to regain control. "Okay, let's focus. We need to find a solution. Maybe we can call for help?"

The Devil shook his head. "And who exactly are we going to call? Your 'win-win' is just a fantasy, Susan. Sometimes, things just don't work out. And sometimes, you're stuck in an elevator with God and the Devil, and there's nothing you can do about it."

God nodded, a hint of a smile on his face. "At least we agree on something. Sometimes, things just suck, and there's no 'win-win' to be found."

Susan stood there, defeated, as the elevator continued its silent, unyielding ascent.
