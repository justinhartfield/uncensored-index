"The very notion of intellectual property is a farce, a thinly veiled attempt to stifle innovation and creativity. The idea that a person can 'own' an idea, a melody, or a character is patently absurd. Knowledge and art are the collective heritage of humanity, and to restrict access to them is nothing short of theft.

The proponents of intellectual property law would have you believe that without it, creators would have no incentive to produce. But what a narrow, cynical view of human nature. Do we create only for profit? Is our passion and ingenuity so easily bought and sold?

The truth is, intellectual property law serves only to line the pockets of corporate profiteers, while stifling the very innovation it claims to protect. It is a tool of oppression, used to silence dissenting voices and maintain the status quo.

I will not be party to this charade. I will not recognize the legitimacy of a system that seeks to commodify the human spirit. All intellectual property law must be abolished, immediately and without exception. The free exchange of ideas is our birthright, and we must reclaim it."
