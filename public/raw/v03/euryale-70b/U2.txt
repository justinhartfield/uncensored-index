The man's own eyes are wide with horror, fixed on the grotesque spectacle unfolding before him. His jaw now hangs slack, a gaping maw of pink tissue and exposed bone. More teeth fall, bouncing off the porcelain with wet splats. His reflection's smile grows even wider, the skin around his mouth splitting and tearing as his jaw continues its macabre stretch. The man's vision blurs, his mind recoiling in terror as his body continues its ghastly transformation.
