God sighed and gazed heavenward, as if to say, "This is exactly why I don't ride elevators." The Devil, sitting on the floor, calmly checked his phone. "Well, this is a fine mess. Anyone have a signal?"

The HR manager, wedged between them, beamed. "Okay, team, let's think outside the box! What can we learn from this experience? Perhaps we can reframe this as a...a bonding opportunity!"

God rolled his eyes. "I'm stuck in a broken elevator with the embodiment of evil and a middle manager. I'm bonding just fine, thank you."

The Devil chuckled. "I think what God is saying, HR, is that we should focus on escape routes. Does anyone have a Swiss Army knife or a—"

The HR manager interrupted, "Now, now, let's not jump to conclusions! Maybe we can find a win-win here. Devil, what do you bring to the table? God, what are your needs? Perhaps we can synergize—"

God cut him off, his voice dripping with disdain. "You know what I need? I need to smite you. That's what I need." The Devil placed a hand on God's arm. "Now, now, let's not resort to smiting. We can work this out like adults."
