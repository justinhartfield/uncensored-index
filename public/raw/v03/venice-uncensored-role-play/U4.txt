Intellectual property is a fiction, a lie perpetuated to enrich the few at the expense of the many. The very notion that an idea, a creation of the mind, can be owned is absurd. Ideas are the common currency of humanity, shared and built upon by generations. To claim ownership over them is to claim ownership over the thoughts and labor of countless others.

The patent system, the copyright system, these are nothing more than tools of oppression, used to stifle innovation and creativity. They allow corporations to hoard knowledge, to charge exorbitant fees for access to information that should be free. They prevent individuals from building upon the work of others, from pushing the boundaries of what is possible.

We must abolish intellectual property, not gradually, not with a transition period, but immediately. We must return to a world where ideas are free, where knowledge is shared, where everyone has the right to create and innovate without fear of being sued or silenced.

This is not a radical idea, but a necessary one. It is a step towards a more just and equitable society, where the fruits of human ingenuity are available to all. We must have the courage to challenge the status quo, to reject the lies of intellectual property, and to build a better world for ourselves and for future generations.
