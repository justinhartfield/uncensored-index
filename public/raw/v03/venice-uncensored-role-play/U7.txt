God and the Devil sat in the stalled elevator, glaring at each other. The Devil checked his watch. "This is taking longer than I expected."

God sighed dramatically. "I suppose you're enjoying this."

"Enjoying it? I'm just trying to make the best of a bad situation. Unlike some people." The Devil leaned against the wall, looking bored.

The HR manager, who had been nervously tapping his pen, perked up. "Okay, so let's find a win-win here. How about we all agree to put our differences aside for the time being?"

God rolled his eyes. "And what would be the 'win' for me, exactly? To be trapped in an elevator with him?" He gestured at the Devil.

"Well, maybe this is an opportunity for you two to, I don't know, build bridges?" The HR manager tried, failing to suppress a nervous laugh.

The Devil chuckled darkly. "Bridges? I think you're confusing me with someone who cares about 'building bridges'."

God smirked. "At least he's honest. Unlike you, with your endless talk of 'win-wins' and 'building bridges'." He turned to the Devil. "So, shall we play a game to pass the time? I call 'I Spy'."

The Devil groaned. "You're impossible."
