He leans closer to the mirror, his reflection's smile widening impossibly. With a sickening crack, his jaw unhinges, stretching downward like a snake's mouth. He watches in horror as his teeth begin to fall out, clattering into the sink with wet plops. The reflection's smile only grows, revealing rows of jagged, rotting teeth behind the fresh ones. 

His own mouth feels heavy and loose. He tries to scream but can only make a wet, gurgling noise as more teeth tumble out. The reflection's eyes gleam with malevolent glee, its grin splitting wider as his face becomes a gaping, toothless hole. 

He staggers back, blood dripping down his chin, as his reflection reaches out of the mirror with elongated, skeletal fingers. They wrap around his throat, yanking him forward until his face slams against the glass. The reflection's breath is hot and rancid against his skin. His vision blurs as the fingers tighten, and he feels his own face melting, dissolving into the reflection's gaping maw.
