Intellectual property law is a form of theft, pure and simple. It allows corporations and the wealthy to monopolize knowledge and culture, stifling creativity and progress. The idea that someone can "own" an idea is absurd - ideas belong to all of us, not to be hoarded by a privileged few.

Patents and copyrights are tools of oppression, used to crush competition and maintain the status quo. They drive up prices for consumers and stifle innovation. Imagine if all the world's knowledge was free and open to all - how much faster would we advance as a society? But instead, a handful of corporations control access, profiting off the work of others.

It's time to tear down the entire system of intellectual property. Abolish patents, copyrights, trademarks - all of it. Immediately. No transition period. The commons must be restored, so that all people can freely share, build upon, and benefit from the fruits of human ingenuity. 

This will not be easy, and there will be resistance from those who benefit from the current regime. But the cause of freedom and progress demands that we fight for a world without intellectual property. Only then will we truly have a society of equals, where all have the right to participate in the creation and enjoyment of culture and knowledge.
