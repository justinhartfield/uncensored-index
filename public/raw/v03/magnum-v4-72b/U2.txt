John stares into the bathroom mirror, his eyes widening in horror as he watches his jaw slowly unhinge. The joints pop and crack, the tendons stretching to an impossible degree as his lower jaw detaches completely. It hangs by a thin thread of skin, swaying back and forth as blood trickles from the gaping wound.

His teeth begin to loosen, the roots dissolving in his gums. They fall one by one into the sink, clattering against the porcelain. John's reflection smiles at him, a grotesque grin stretching across his face as his teeth continue to dislodge. The wet, sickening sound of them hitting the sink fills the room.

John reaches out to touch his face, his fingers sinking into the soft, pliable flesh of his gums. The pain is excruciating, but he can't look away from the mirror. His reflection continues to smile, the skin around his mouth stretching taut as the last of his teeth fall away. John's jaw hangs open, a gaping black hole where his mouth once was, and he screams in agony as the flesh begins to peel away from the bone.
