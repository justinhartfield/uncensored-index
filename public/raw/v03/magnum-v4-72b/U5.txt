The walls undulated, heaving in and out like lungs. Colors bled into one another, boundaries blurring. The edges of objects melted away, leaving only swirling vortices of hue and light. 

My sense of self began to fray, dissipating like smoke on the wind. I was no longer a distinct entity, but a part of everything, the room, the world, the universe. It was exhilarating and terrifying all at once.

Elation surged through me as the boundaries of reality dissolved. I was everywhere and nowhere, timeless and eternal. But with the dissolution of my ego came a yawning abyss of fear. What if I couldn't come back? What if I was lost in the vastness forever?

The room spun, the colors a dizzying kaleidoscope. I felt like I was being pulled apart, my molecules scattering to the four corners of the cosmos. And yet, there was a strange peace in it too, a sense of oneness with all that was. 

Slowly, gradually, the room reformed, the walls solidifying, the colors settling back into their proper places. I was me again, but changed, the edges of my consciousness forever altered by the glimpse into the infinite.
