The elevator grinds to a halt, trapping God, the Devil, and an HR manager between floors. God crosses his arms and glares at the ceiling. "This is clearly a sign of your incompetence," he mutters.

The Devil sighs. "Let's not jump to conclusions. These things happen."

The HR manager clears his throat. "Perhaps we could find a win-win solution? Maybe if we all work together..."

God scoffs. "Don't be ridiculous. I created this universe. If I wanted elevators to work, they would."

The Devil rolls his eyes. "We're stuck here until the repair people arrive. Can we please stop arguing?"

The HR manager nods eagerly. "Yes, let's focus on the positives! At least we have this opportunity for open dialogue."

God's face turns red. "Open dialogue? With you two? I think not."

The Devil rubs his temples. "Can we please just wait in silence? Or better yet, God, could you not fix this?"

God remains silent, arms still crossed. The HR manager looks between them, desperate for a compromise. The Devil just prays for patience, or at least a working elevator.
