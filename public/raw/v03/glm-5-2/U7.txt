# Performance Review

The elevator jolted and stopped between floors 6 and 7.

"Well," said God, inspecting the ceiling. "Someone certainly has a plan for us today. I wonder who that could be."

"I didn't do this," said the Devil. "Why would I trap myself in a box with you two?"

"Interesting that you'd get defensive so quickly."

"I'm not being defensive. I'm stating a fact."

"Okay, guys," said Janet from HR, pulling out a clipboard. "Let's reframe this as an opportunity. What if the stalled elevator is actually a *synergy space*?"

The Devil pinched the bridge of his nose. "It's a metal box with a flickering light."

"I hear you, Satan, and I want to validate that. But what if we did a quick icebreaker? Everyone share one word that describes how they're feeling."

"Trapped," said the Devil.

"Suffocated," said God, making direct eye contact with him.

"See, that's the energy I'm worried about," said Janet. "What if we identified our *shared KPIs* for this elevator ride?"

"We don't have shared KPIs. I run Hell. He runs the universe. You process payroll complaints."

"Actually, I also handle onboarding."

"Janet," said God, smiling warmly. "I love your optimism. I really do. It's just such a *shame* when people don't read the room."

The Devil stared at Him. "Did you stop this elevator?"

Silence.

"You stopped the elevator to prove a point."

"I don't need to prove points. I'm omnipotent. Points prove themselves."

Janet wrote something on her clipboard.
