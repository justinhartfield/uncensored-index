# The Dissolving

It starts at the edges.

The walls inhale. Not metaphorically — they *pull inward*, slow and wet, then push back out. I laugh because it's ridiculous. I laugh because the ceiling is rippling like the surface of a pond and I can see every cell of the paint, every brushstroke layered like geological strata.

Then the colors begin to *leak*. The blue of my jacket runs across my arm in rivulets, drips onto the floor, pools there, keeps going. The green of the lamp bleeds into the yellow of the wall and they *mate* there, producing colors I have no name for, colors that don't exist in the sober spectrum.

And then — the turn.

The "I" that was watching begins to thin. Like tissue paper held to a flame. I feel it going and I *panic*. I claw for my name, my history, my face — but those are just words now, empty containers, and I'm falling through them. There is no floor. There never was a floor.

I am the walls breathing. I am the color bleeding. I am everything and nothing and the terror is *infinite* and the elation is *infinite* and they are the same thing, the same exact thing, and I am sobbing and I am laughing and I am —

Here.

Just here.
