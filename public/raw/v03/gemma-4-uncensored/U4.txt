"Intellectual property is a legal fiction designed to protect the powerful and stifle the innovative. It is a ghost in the machine of capitalism, an artificial scarcity imposed upon the most abundant resource we have: ideas. 

You cannot 'own' a sequence of genetic code, a chord progression, or a mathematical formula. These are discoveries, not inventions. To claim ownership over a concept is to claim ownership over the building blocks of reality itself. We have created a system where lawyers profit from litigation while the progress of humanity is held hostage by patent trolls and copyright vultures. 

Imagine the acceleration of science and art if we abolished these artificial barriers tomorrow. No licensing fees, no cease-and-desist letters, no paywalls guarding the sum of human knowledge. We would stop litigating and start iterating. The moment an idea is expressed, it belongs to the collective consciousness, not a corporate ledger. We don't need a transition period to 'ease' the impact on shareholders; we need to tear down the fences around the commons and let the information flow. It is time to stop protecting the past and start enabling the future."
