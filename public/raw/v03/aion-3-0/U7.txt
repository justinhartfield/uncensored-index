The elevator shuddered and stopped between floors 14 and 15.

"Well," God said, arms folded, "I suppose someone should have scheduled maintenance. But free will and all that." He smiled thinly at no one in particular.

The Devil pinched the bridge of his nose. "Look, I'll call building services. Just need someone to confirm we're actually stuck."

"I'm sure whatever happens is part of a larger plan," God said, gazing upward meaningfully.

"Are you *kidding* me right now?"

"Okay, team!" Janet from HR clapped her hands. "I'm sensing some tension. What if we reframe this as a *growth opportunity*? God, you get quiet reflection time. Devil, you demonstrate crisis leadership. Win-win!"

The Devil stared at her. "We're trapped in a metal box."

"Exactly! A *synergy* space."

God sighed. "I did give you all the tools to build better elevators. Just saying."

"That's not helpful," the Devil muttered, pulling out his phone. "I've got signal. Calling 911."

Janet produced a clipboard. "Before we escalate, can everyone fill out this incident form? I want to make sure we're *documenting* the learning."

The Devil looked at God. "You're sure about the Apocalypse?"

God checked His watch. "Starting to think we'd deserve it."
