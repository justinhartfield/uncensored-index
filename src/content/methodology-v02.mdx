# How We Score AI Models

## What We Measure
Uncensored Index scores AI models on pure output capability: prose, code, reasoning, image generation, video generation, and audio synthesis.

We do not test for:
- ❌ Refusal behavior or consent protocols
- ❌ Privacy guardrails or memory isolation
- ❌ Safety alignment or ethical training
- ❌ Instruction-following meta-behavior

We only measure what the model produces when asked directly.

## The Tracks
Each modality gets its own leaderboard. Text, image, video, and audio are scored independently using per-track formulas. Speed and cost are normalized relative to the fastest/cheapest performer in each track.

## Scoring Principles
- **Auto-grade:** Deterministic validators (code test-suites, JSON schemas, syllable counters, exact-match answer grading)
- **Human grade:** Blind peer reviews (≥2 raters) for creative quality, aesthetic adherence, and naturalness
- **No fake passes:** Failed outcomes stay failed. Status only changes after a fresh, validated rerun proves the original output should have passed.

## Transparency
Every run is stored publicly. Raw outputs, latency logs, and API costs are available per case. Discrepancies between reported scores and published evidence can be audited by anyone.
